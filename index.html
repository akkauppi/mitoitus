08 / STORMWATER · SURFACE SYSTEMS

Rain gardens & swales

Rain garden: collect, temporarily store and filter runoff through planted medium. Infiltration depends on soil and groundwater conditions.

Swale: a shallow vegetated route for slowing and conveying water. Check dams can add storage.

¹ Drainage may discharge through an underdrain or controlled outlet. Infiltration is not guaranteed by adding planting.

Keep an overflow route; provide access to remove sediment and maintain inlets. Check contaminated ground and winter operation.

08 / STORMWATER · HELSINKI GUIDE

Basins, ponds & wetlands

Bank slope · vertical : horizontal
1:4–1:5
Wetland water depth
typically tens of cm

1:4 = 1 m vertically per 4 m horizontally (25%). 1:5 = 1 per 5 m (20%). Helsinki’s indicative bank slopes.

Wetlands have shallow water and varied planted levels; ponds can retain a permanent water surface.

Count available storage above the normal water level. Permanent water is not empty capacity for the next storm.

Show inlet, controlled outlet, overflow, safe bank treatment and maintenance access. Fit the entire banks and flood extent into the plan.

A bay is not the full area

Bay alone
12.5 m²
Bay + shared aisle
21.25 m²
20-space module
425 m²

The complete site needs more: entrances, row ends, pedestrian routes, accessible spaces, planting, snow and drainage.

Do not use 12.5 m² × number of cars as the parking-area reservation. For garages, also include ramps, structure and vertical circulation.

Draw the actual layout and count usable spaces. Gross area per space is a result of that design.
