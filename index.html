03 / TURNING SPACE

Room to turn.

SuRaKu’s outdoor guidance uses at least 1.5 m for a wheelchair and 2.5 m for a powered wheelchair.

A turning circle is clear space, not the vehicle outline. Keep furniture and door swings out of the usable turning area.

Check the actual device for larger mobility scooters or other equipment; the source does not give one universal scooter turning diameter.

06 / CHECK THE WHOLE STREET

The sum is
only the start.

  1. Check the section.Add kerbs, buffers, furniture, drainage, edges and snow space where needed.
  2. Check the plan.Stops, crossings, entries, turning paths and loading need length as well as width.
  3. Check the vertical space.Include canopy, soil, utilities, clear height and overhead wires.
  4. Check each overlap.A shared area needs a workable operating arrangement. Avoid counting the same space twice.
A positive remainder is unallocated space, not proof of a complete or compliant design.

Make the choices
visible.

  • Can spaces or functions be combined?
  • Is the same solution needed along the entire street?
