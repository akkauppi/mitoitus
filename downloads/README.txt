MITOITUS — CAD reference templates

Open the DXF files in a CAD editor at 1:1 in the declared units.
Metres: INSUNITS 6. Millimetres: INSUNITS 4. International feet: INSUNITS 2.
One international foot is exactly 0.3048 m; this is not the former US survey foot.
Geometry and annotations are converted together. DXF coordinates are rounded to
four decimal places of the selected unit. Finnish source precision is unchanged.
SVG files are convenient vector previews; their viewBox uses the selected units,
but browser pixel dimensions do not establish a physical print scale.

Accessible: two clear turning circles, route/passing strips, bench-side space,
and the SuRaKu courtyard accessible bay. Route-strip length 3 m and bench-space
depth 1.5 m are illustrative; only their labelled widths are source dimensions.
Rescue: full reference circles with inner/outer radii and the aerial set-up area.
These are NOT swept paths or driveable-route demonstrations. HIKLU's additional
driving/boom allowances have not been added. Verify the actual vehicle and route.
Street: separate width strips, each drawn 20 m long for comparison only.
They do not prescribe a combined street cross-section.

Sources, editions, assumptions and formulae: evidence.html and evidence.json.
Online: https://akkauppi.github.io/mitoitus/evidence.html#cad
English synthesis and new drawings are assistant drafts for teaching.
Municipal original documents remain authoritative for their stated applications.
