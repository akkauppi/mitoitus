BUILT-FORM

Building types and boundaries

The TKK reference lists slab, point, small apartment, row and detached types, and distinguishes plot, block and district floor-area ratios. New drawings are schematic. Equal floor area in different forms follows from summing the floor plates; it does not establish equal urban quality.

  • TKK / YKS — Mitoitus — Talotyypit; Tehokkuusluvut. 28 Aug 2007
DENSITY

Floor-area ratio

e = sum of counted floor areas / selected land area. Illustrative equal-floor calculation: 1,000 × 4 / 5,000 = 0.80. Floor-count and land-area inputs are assumptions, not permissions or a regulatory definition of counted floor space.

  • TKK / YKS — Mitoitus — Tehokkuusluvut. 28 Aug 2007
SCALE

Model conversion

A metre is 1,000 millimetres. At scale 1:n, model mm = real m × 1,000 / n. Thus 3.5 m at 1:500 is 7 mm. The initial scale is an example, not the workshop’s asserted scale. The paper/cardboard instruction is verbatim supplied English.

  • Supplied W4 English instructions — selected extract — Source paragraphs 18–19. 2026 course source
WALKING

Footway and clear widths

Espoo: 2.5 m basic footway. SuRaKu card 1: 1.5 m clear passage, 1.8 m for two wheelchairs passing and 2.3 m for machine maintenance. Distinguish total route width from unobstructed passage.

  • Espoo — Katupoikkileikkausten suunnitteluohjeet — Printed p. 18, §5.1. 2010
  • SuRaKu — Esteetön ympäristö, cards 1–8 — Card 1, PDF p. 1. 6 Feb 2008
CYCLING

Cycling arrangements

Espoo: shared route 3–4 m; separated walking/cycling total 4 m, main routes at least 4.5 m; one-way cycle lane 1.75 m, with a 0.75 m minimum extra buffer if adjacent to parking. These are different facility types; retain their context.

  • Espoo — Katupoikkileikkausten suunnitteluohjeet — Printed p. 18, §§5.2–5.4. 2010
STAYING

Gradients, furniture and parking

SuRaKu route crossfall: special/basic level 2%/3%; longitudinal gradient 5%/8%; ramps separate. Furniture belongs beside the route. Espoo 811A excludes cycle/scooter parking in the 5 m before a crossing, cycle crossing or intersecting cycle track. Capacity and handling space depend on layout.

  • SuRaKu — Esteetön ympäristö, cards 1–8 — Card 1, gradients; card 2, furniture. 6 Feb 2008
  • Helsinki — Kalusteiden sijoittaminen — Cycle-rack placement and handling space. Retrieved 12 Nov 2026
  • Espoo — Pyöräpysäköinti pysäkillä, 5222/811A — Notes beside the five layouts. 9 Oct 2024
TURNING

Wheelchair turning circles

SuRaKu: clear turning diameter at least 1.5 m for a wheelchair, 2.5 m for a powered wheelchair. The sheet draws them at a common scale. Keep the usable envelope clear (editorial application of clear-space guidance); do not infer that every mobility scooter fits this envelope.

  • SuRaKu — Esteetön ympäristö, cards 1–8 — Card 3, PDF p. 5; also card 4 p. 7 and card 7 p. 13. 6 Feb 2008
RAMPS

Passing and ramp landings

SuRaKu: route clear width 1.5 m, passing 1.8 m. Ramp recommendation ≤5%, maximum 8%; on long ramps >5%, straight intermediate landings ≥2 m every 6 m. The separate ramp width and turn details remain in the source. Example: run = rise / gradient = 0.30/0.05 = 6 m, excluding landings.

  • SuRaKu — Esteetön ympäristö, cards 1–8 — Cards 1–3; ramp profile and text on PDF pp. 5–6. 6 Feb 2008
ARRIVAL

Seating and accessible parking

SuRaKu: wheelchair space ≥0.9 m at a bench end; backs, armrests and varied seat heights. Courtyard accessible bay ≥3.6 × 5 m, gradient ≤2% both directions. Kerbside bays and vehicle transfer/lift access need separate layouts. The sentence about joining shared spaces is an editorial design prompt.

  • SuRaKu — Esteetön ympäristö, cards 1–8 — Card 2, furniture; card 4, PDF pp. 7–8. 6 Feb 2008
  • Espoo — Liikkumisesteisen pysäköintipaikka kadulla, 5222/028A — Separate kerbside accessible-parking drawing. Assignment-linked drawing
TRANSIT

Tram corridors and bus-stop lengths

Espoo light-rail reservation ≥8 m, stops separately; Helsinki’s draft tram card gives ≥6 m on a line section. They are not the same reservation. Espoo 031D gives 16 m for one bus, 32 m for two, using a 15 m bus; additional approach, junction and crossing clearances are shown separately.

  • Espoo — Katupoikkileikkausten suunnitteluohjeet — Printed p. 17, §4.7. 2010
  • Helsinki — Raitiotiet — Periaateratkaisu ja mitoitus; source marked draft. 8 May 2024
  • Espoo — Ajoratapysäkki, 5222/031D — Layouts A–C and junction-clearance details. 9 Oct 2024
VEHICLES

Local collector and essential vehicle functions

Espoo local-collector lane 3.0–3.25 m; when buses govern, ≥3.25 m. Turning depends on the vehicle and junction. The W4 brief lists servicing, deliveries, waste collection and rescue access, and excludes long-term private-car parking. Turning sketch is illustrative, not a computed path.

  • Espoo — Katupoikkileikkausten suunnitteluohjeet — Printed pp. 15–16, §4.4. 2010
  • Espoo — Tontin ajoliittymä, 5222/814 — Principle 2, design-vehicle check. 23 Apr 2025
  • Helsinki — Katuliittymä — Junction principles. 8 May 2024
  • Supplied W4 English instructions — selected extract — Theme 4. 2026 course source
RESCUE

Rescue geometry

HIKLU: fire engine and aerial appliance route width 3.5 m, clear height 4.2 m. Inner/outer radii: engine 5/10.5 m; aerial 5.5/12.5 m. Aerial set-up width ≥6 m, target 8 m, length 13 m. The vehicle models also specify driving allowance 0.4 m and aerial boom allowance 1.0 m. Our circles do not add these allowances or simulate a manoeuvre. Check loads, slopes, reach, trees, wires and snow against the complete guide.

  • HIKLU — Pelastustien suunnittelu ja toteutus — pp. 7–13, §§4.1–5.3. 23 Apr 2024 · v1.3
TREES

Tree soil volume

Helsinki’s draft tree-and-tram card gives approximately 25 m³ conventional soil for a large-growing tree and discusses 40–60 m³ structural soil because stone displaces water storage. These are contextual indicative volumes, not a universal minimum. Coordinate roots, utilities and mature crown; grouped trees should have continuous soil.

  • Helsinki — Katupuut ja raitioliikenne, mitoitusohje — Puiden kasvualustan mitoitus; source marked draft. 8 May 2024
  • Helsinki — Kasvualustat — Mitoitus. 8 May 2024
  • Helsinki — Katu- ja puistopuut — Tree growing conditions. 23 Jan 2026
STORMWATER

Detention example

Espoo’s Kaavoitus section gives an example zoning provision: 1 m³ per 100 m² impermeable surface, emptying in 12–24 h and controlled overflow. It is not asserted for this site. Example calculation: 600/100 = 6 m³; at assumed 0.20 m effective depth, 6/0.20 = 30 m² idealised plan area. Side slopes, freeboard, ground conditions and actual inflow/outflow are not calculated.

  • Espoo — Hulevesi — Hulevesien hallinta kaupungissa → Kaavoitus. Retrieved 12 Nov 2026
GROUND

Planting strips and shared ground

Espoo planting widths: trees ≥3 m (main streets ≥3.5 m); lawn/ground cover ≥2 m. These do not prove adequate soil volume. The checks for utilities, snow, sightlines, overhead wires and salt are a synthesis of the listed guides.

  • Espoo — Katupoikkileikkausten suunnitteluohjeet — Printed pp. 10–11, 19. 2010
  • Helsinki — Katu- ja puistopuut — Growing conditions and salt. 23 Jan 2026
  • Helsinki — Katupuut ja raitioliikenne, mitoitusohje — Trees near tram infrastructure. 8 May 2024
  • HIKLU — Pelastustien suunnittelu ja toteutus — Obstacles and winter maintenance. 23 Apr 2024 · v1.3
BUDGET

Width budget and strip templates

Total = Σ(width × count); remainder = available − total. Default: 2×2.5 + 2×1.75 + 2×3 + 2×3.25 = 21 m, leaving 3 m of an assumed 24 m street. Optional tram uses 8 m. The combination, street width, strip lengths and zero initial buffer are editorial assumptions; this is arithmetic, not a validated cross-section. The CAD export treats all rows as separate adjacent strips and makes no sharing allowance.

  • Espoo — Katupoikkileikkausten suunnitteluohjeet — Default widths: printed pp. 16–19. 2010
  • Supplied W4 English instructions — selected extract — Separate needs must be negotiated in the shared model. 2026 course source
INTEGRATION

Checks beyond the sum

Editorial synthesis: a cross-section sum does not establish junction operation, loading space, accessible continuity, below-ground compatibility, canopy clearance or a rescue arrangement. Check sections, plans and vertical envelopes together. Shared use must be justified rather than subtracted automatically.

CAD

CAD units, templates and limits

All canonical geometry is stored in metres. DXF coordinates are multiplied by 1 for metres, 1,000 for millimetres or 1/0.3048 for international feet. DXF $INSUNITS is respectively 6, 4 or 2. Files use R2000 DXF. Coordinates and text are rounded to four decimals in the selected unit. SVG is a vector preview with user units matching the selection, not a physical-size print template. The evidence above is the proof for geometry; exact conversion does not make the source values more precise.

  • NIST — Revised Unit Conversion Factors — Conversion table, foot row. 3 Feb 2026

The original reference

The TKK / YKS page is dated 28 August 2007. Its HTML and content drawings have been preserved locally with URLs, retrieval metadata and SHA-256 checksums. The original includes building rules, parking layouts and recreation-area quantities; these historical numbers are not promoted as current guidance here. Its light-traffic and public-service sections were unfinished, and no missing teaching content has been invented.

The new helper extends the source’s compact visual-reference approach with the W4 assignment’s municipal sources, accessible movement and downloadable reference geometry. Archived originals are separate from this public repository.

Source discrepancies retained

  • The Espoo stormwater page labels a link “2025–2034” while one target is the 2020 programme; another inspected target is a water-services development plan. The helper’s numerical claim comes only from the verified Kaavoitus passage on the page, not from a claimed 2025–2034 programme PDF.
  • The workbook’s Helsinki street-space PDF is dated May 2014. A growing-medium card refers to May 2015. They have not been treated as the same edition.
  • The Helsinki tram and tree/tram cards are marked draft by their publisher. Their values remain labelled with that context.
  • SuRaKu is the 2008 collection linked by the assignment. Its recommendations are not presented as a complete statement of current building regulations.
